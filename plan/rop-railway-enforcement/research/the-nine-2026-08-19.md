## ⛔ Two deductions the count concealed

**1. Overseer's 10 are FIVE functions counted twice.** Every one appears at an identical
path and line under both `overseer/` and `.claude-plugin/overseer/`. Verified by `cmp -s`:
`foreman_gather_sources.py` and `codex_sessions.py` are **byte-identical** across the two
trees, which is what `just check-codex-plugin-runnable-launcher` enforces. Convert the five
primaries and re-sync; the copies follow for free.

**2. One of beads-fabro's three is not product code.** `identity-probe.py:71 main` lives
under `plan/beads-v1-1-2-upgrade/rehearsal-package/wrappers/` — one of the three scratch
scripts identified in the fleet-wide universe audit. **Converting a rehearsal wrapper to
the railway would be absurd**; it is in the universe by path, not by intent.

## Suggested sequencing, with the reason

**`livespec-runtime`'s two first.** `parse_depends_on_entry` and `load_github_app_config`
are in a repo with no mirror tree, no `plan/` scratch in its universe, and the smallest
universe of the three (39). It is the cleanest place to prove the loop — the local-inference
route, the Red→Green ritual, the batching discipline — before touching overseer, where every
conversion lands twice and the mirror check must be re-synced in the same change.

**Not asserted:** that these nine are *easy*. Each still needs its failure type named
before the worker sees it (per the pilot), and `run_json_command` / `read_journal` are I/O
seams whose failure payloads are design decisions, not mechanical ones.
