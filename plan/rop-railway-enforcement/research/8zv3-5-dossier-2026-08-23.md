## 1. The question, in one sentence

`check-public-api-result-typed` skips every file whose NAME begins with `_`. **Is that
skip authorised by the ratified rule** — which convicts on `_`-prefixed *names* — **or is
it an unratified narrowing that removes hundreds of functions from a rule's reach?**

## 2. The rule argument — and what honestly survives of it

**FOR names-not-files:** clause 0 adopts §"Typechecker rule set"'s private-helper
definition, which reads *"single-leading-underscore prefix **or not in `__all__`**"* and
quantifies over **functions and dataclass fields**. There is no module- or file-level
clause anywhere in it. Clause 1 names the consumption unit explicitly as the **module
boundary**, which a `_`-prefixed module's `__all__`-listed functions cross like any other.

**The load-bearing evidence** is `commands/_config.py` in beads-fabro: a `_`-prefixed file
declaring six public names in `__all__`, imported by 17 non-test product modules, never
once looked at by the check that governs them.

⚠️ **The proponent's own verb is too strong, and its author said so.** The addendum claims
the ratified text *contradicts* a file skip, leaning on §"Module API surface"'s "EVERY
module MUST declare `__all__`". That sentence is scoped to
`.claude-plugin/scripts/livespec/**`. ▶️ **"Does not AUTHORISE" is the defensible verb;
"contradicts" is not.** The surviving claim is an argument from absence — still fatal for a
skip of this size, but a more modest claim than the one first written.

## 5. Recommendation — separate the RULE from the ENFORCEMENT BASIS

This is Counter 3's surviving form, and it is the recommendation:

> **Accept names-not-files as the RULE. Keep the current arming basis as the BASIS.
> They are separable, and the thread's notes conflate them more than they should.**

Why this is the right shape:

1. **The rule reading is sound and the costing does not bear on it.** Whether a file skip
   is authorised by the ratified text is a question about the text. 606 functions say how
   much the answer is worth, not what it is.
2. **Ratifying 612 buys mostly QUESTIONS, not work.** ~93% of convictions under either
   basis are questions rather than conversions (`first-unit-recheck-2026-08-19.md`). The
   expansion adds ~400 open questions and almost no additional actionable conversions.
3. **`46c5dab` is the precedent, and it cost a P0.** Widening this check ahead of adoption
   turned five repos red and was reverted (`f4247110`). The standing constraint —
   *adoption BEFORE arming* — applies to a basis change exactly as it applied to the
   decoupling.
4. **The cost is CONCENTRATED, so it can be sequenced.** Two repos carry **94%** of the
   delta (overseer 404 + beads-fabro 164 = 568 of 606). **Six of nine members move by 18
   or fewer**, and three move by zero. ▶️ A per-repo sequence is available that never
   turns more than one member red at a time.

## 6. What the panel should ask for before ratifying

1. **An independently-IMPLEMENTED re-derivation** of the WITHOUT-skip figure — §4 supplies
   reproducibility, not correctness.
2. **A cross-module-boundary check across the delta.** The `_config.py` evidence is one
   file deep (17 importers). Counter 5 fails on magnitude, but nobody has verified that the
   other ~600 are consumed across module boundaries the way `_config.py` is. **If a large
   share are genuinely module-private, the rule reading survives but the costing shrinks.**
3. **A decision on overseer's mirror** — 276 files, only 44 under the identity check. It is
   40% of the fleet's WITHOUT-skip total and it is not obvious it should be counted at all.
