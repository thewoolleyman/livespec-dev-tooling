## Method

- `data/measurement-scripts.md` (inventory-dump) read every tenant with `bd list --status all --limit 0 --json` and, per plan directory,
  took the epic from `associated_work_item_id` and its children as the UNION of the dotted-id prefix, the `parent`
  field and the `parent-child` edge (CLAUDE.md trap catalogue).
- Three readers then classified each plan against the report item keys (`data/report-item-keys.md`) from its
  charter, its `next_action` and its open children's titles. Their full per-plan records, with carrier ids, are
  `data/inventory-livespec-dev-tooling.md` (37 plans), `data/inventory-livespec-orchestrator-beads-fabro.md`
  (45 plans) and `data/inventory-other-repositories.md` (12 repositories, 41 plan directories).
- Limits, stated so nobody reads more into it: a child is assigned from its TITLE unless the per-plan record says
  it was read; the homelab and openbrain ledgers could not be read from hetzner-xubuntu (their credential wrappers
  are not installed here), so those two are classified from plan directories only; `updated_at` shows a bulk touch
  on 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-12, so dormancy is judged from `last_session` and the `next_action`.
- Seats were read once at ~21:55Z (`herdr agent list` on hetzner-xubuntu and on the VPS). The coordinator has since
  directed that this seat does not drive herdr on the VPS, so the seat column is a 21:55Z reading, not live state.
