### B. Flake retries withheld (coordinator, from gmktec-factory-placement's measurement)

The flake-retry pytest plugin withholds the in-place retry for a test whose closure it cannot read ("an
indeterminable path in a closure module", `livespec_dev_tooling/flake_retry_decisions.py`). Each such flake costs a
whole-job rerun: livespec-dev-tooling#4545 lost two consecutive suite runs (1:30:37) to two untouched
incident-module tests.

- **Owner: fleet-flaky-tests (livespec-dev-tooling-zwveqp)**, which owns the plugin. Existing carriers:
  livespec-dev-tooling-k3lpwj (the closure reads paths built through getattr, an aliased request, an imported
  fixture; pending-approval), wc23r2, d4siza, n3dr3z, o4hkgh (each a different withholding cause) and ln77b6 (a
  withheld retry prints no marker). No new item is needed; k3lpwj is the carrier for this case if its owner
  confirms the incident modules fall under it.
- **Measured bound**: whole-run reruns (`run_attempt > 1`) over 7 d were 43 runs and 1,372 runner-minutes in
  livespec-dev-tooling and 37 runs and 3,381 minutes in the orchestrator (data pack A). Withheld flake retries
  are a subset of those 80 reruns; how large a subset is not measured, because the plugin's
  `FLAKE-RETRY-WITHHELD` warning is only in job logs. So the ceiling is 80 delayed PRs a week across the two
  repositories, each delayed by one suite run; none is lost to the metric.
- **Rank: 11**, below the rows that change PR counts or return a third of CI minutes. fleet-flaky-tests joins the
  track for this row only.
